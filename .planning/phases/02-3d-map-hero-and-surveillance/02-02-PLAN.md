## Phase Goal

**As a** David (the only user), **I want to** open Shinigami Eyes and watch the red eyes activate onto a dark, tilted 3D map of my surroundings with named tags, info cards and the cameras around me, **so that** I can read my street at a glance, Watch Dogs-style, without any third party learning where I am.

This plan (slice): "the HUD always comes up and always shows the right place". Fixes the Phase 1 review warnings that Phase 2 depends on (RESEARCH Pitfall 10): WR-06 (first paint hangs on IndexedDB, a precondition for HUD-03 "never delays data"), WR-04 (stale-location hybrid profile, which would put camera tags in the wrong place), WR-07 (stuck loading / missing empty state), WR-08 (GPS timeout reported as off), WR-03 (HTTP errors shown as OFF-GRID). Also records GPS accuracy for the accuracy ring (D-17).

Purpose: the map, marker and camera layers built later all ride on cached-first boot and correct location state.
Output: `timeout.ts`, `HttpError`, hardened `app.ts`/`location.ts`, idempotent `NextList.setState`, `app.test.ts`.
</objective>
