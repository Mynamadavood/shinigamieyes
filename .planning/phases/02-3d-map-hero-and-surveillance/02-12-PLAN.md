## Phase Goal

**As a** David (the only user), **I want to** open Shinigami Eyes and watch the red eyes activate onto a dark, tilted 3D map of my surroundings with named tags, info cards and the cameras around me, **so that** I can read my street at a glance, Watch Dogs-style, without any third party learning where I am.

This plan (slice): "the eyes activate". The HUD-03 hero, implemented as DOM/SVG/CSS only (one WebGL context rule), with a hard 1500 ms cap, tap-to-skip, cold-open-only detection and a reduced-motion path (UI-SPEC "Hero Activation").

Discretion choice (CONTEXT "Claude's Discretion: animation timings"): the UI-SPEC's stroke-dashoffset draw-on is replaced by a translateX mask reveal, because RESEARCH Pattern 9 / Pitfall 7 show dashoffset animates on the main thread, which MapLibre init blocks; all hero motion is transform/opacity so it stays smooth.

Purpose: Light Yagami "eyes switching on" moment without ever costing the < 1 s cached paint.
Output: hero.ts, hero-art.ts, hero.css, tests, boot wiring.
</objective>
