<objective>
## Phase Goal

**As a** David (the only user), **I want to** open Shinigami Eyes and watch the red eyes activate onto a dark, tilted 3D map of my surroundings with named tags, info cards and the cameras around me, **so that** I can read my street at a glance, Watch Dogs-style, without any third party learning where I am.

This plan (slice): "the 3D map appears under the HUD". MapLibre 6.16 with a hand-authored style generated from the CSS tokens on the Protomaps v4 schema (RESEARCH Pattern 3: `buildings.height/min_height`, NOT `render_height`), self-hosted glyphs, loaded lazily so cached-first paint stays under 1 s, and wired to the location state.

Deviations from the approved UI-SPEC, all forced by MapLibre/Protomaps facts (RESEARCH Pitfalls 1-3) and accepted by the orchestrator: extrusion minzoom 15 (not 14; buildings below z15 are merged blobs), extrusion opacity 1 layer-wide (not 0.92; opacity is not data-driven), height properties `height`/`min_height`.

Purpose: HUD-04 look and HUD-10 same-origin tiles/glyphs on the phone.
Output: `web/src/map/{types,style,view,map}.ts` + tests, `map.css`, committed glyph PBFs, app hook, vite proxy/precache globs.
</objective>
