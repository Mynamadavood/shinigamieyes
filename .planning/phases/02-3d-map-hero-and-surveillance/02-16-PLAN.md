## Phase Goal

**As a** David (the only user), **I want to** open Shinigami Eyes and watch the red eyes activate onto a dark, tilted 3D map of my surroundings with named tags, info cards and the cameras around me, **so that** I can read my street at a glance, Watch Dogs-style, without any third party learning where I am.

This plan closes UAT gap 2 (major): David flipped PROVISIONAL decision 2 on 2026-11-10 ("show if legally possible"; chose guards + business owner names). Guard posts are mapped places (published OSM data), not people; nothing on the card identifies a person.

Design (Claude's discretion, stated): guard posts get their own record kind `k = "guard"` (item group `guard`, tag name `Guard post`) served by the existing CCTV provider (150 m layer `cameras_cctv`), so the count readout covers them and no new layer, icon or SVG symbol is needed (the tag reuses the `cctv` icon via iconFor's default). The spoken aria-label for guard tags (`tags.ts` spokenType) is updated in plan 02-17, which owns tags.ts in wave 2.

Output: updated weekly job, provider and card; tests. The CT job re-run happens in plan 02-18.
</objective>
