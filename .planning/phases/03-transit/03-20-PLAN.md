Tapping a row opens the stop card with up to 2 departures per route and direction; uncatchable lines are dimmed with `too late`.
    3. Vehicles (TRAN-03): small blue tram/bus icons with route numbers move toward your stops. Estimated trams have a dashed frame and `~96`, and their card says `TRAM 96 · EST`. Tapping one shows which of your stops it is heading to, with a countdown.
    4. Disruptions (TRAN-04): if any are on your routes, the corner shows `N disruptions · your routes`, and tapping it lists them. Affected rows and cards show a warning mark, red only for a stopped service. If none exist today, write "none on my routes".

    6. Countdown format (David to confirm, not a blocker): rows show zero-padded `06:12` / `leave in 01:40`. Keep this, or ask for `6:12`?

Built instead: only the soonest row in the leave-now window turns red with LEAVE NOW; any other row in the window reads `leave now` in white, so two red countdowns never compete (UI-SPEC red-scarcity rule). If you say no: every row in the window turns red (`leaveNowRed` set on all of them in web/src/model.ts).
    Expected: items 1-5 pass. Items 6-8 are your preference.
