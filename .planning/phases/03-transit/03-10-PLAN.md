## Phase Goal

**As** David, **I want to** see how to get moving from where I stand (nearest stops, live departures, approaching vehicles and disruptions), **so that** I am always one step ahead of the next tram, train or bus.

This plan (slice 2, stop card half) fills the stop card with departures. It shows groups per route and direction, 2 lines each and 8 lines at most, with LIVE/SCHED marks, delay and platform notes, dimmed uncatchable lines, the CANCELLED chip and all the loading/no-key/error/empty/stale states. The card's countdowns tick once a second through the existing ticker, and an open card refreshes when new departures arrive.

Purpose: TRAN-02 detail view (D-06, D-07).
Output: card.ts sections + ticking, transit-card departures, tags.ts refresh-on-context, tests.
