## Phase Goal

**As** David, **I want to** see how to get moving from where I stand (nearest stops, live departures, approaching vehicles and disruptions), **so that** I am always one step ahead of the next tram, train or bus.

This plan (slice 1, server data half) builds the stop dataset and the nearest-stop selection that TRAN-01 runs on. Neither needs a key. It builds `transit_stops.json` from the public GTFS Schedule download and gives the server an in-memory lat-bucket index, following the CameraStore pattern. Shapely STRtree is not installed and no new packages are allowed.

Purpose: TRAN-01 works with no PTV key at all.
Output: refresh job, TransitStore, tests with tiny generated GTFS zips.

`nearby(lat, lon)` works per mode:
