## Phase Goal

**As** David, **I want to** see how to get moving from where I stand (nearest stops, live departures, approaching vehicles and disruptions), **so that** I am always one step ahead of the next tram, train or bus.

This plan (slice 4, server half) adds vehicles approaching David's stops. Real bus positions come from the departures response. Trams get a labelled estimate, placed between the two stops the run is travelling between according to the stopping-pattern times (D-20). Each source sits behind a `VehicleSource` seam, so GTFS-R can replace the estimates later. The output is the `transit_vehicles` layer.

Purpose: TRAN-03 on the server.
Output: vehicles.py, provider, wiring, tests.
</objective>

- `class VehicleSource(Protocol): def vehicles(self, stops: list[NearStop], cache: Callable[[int], dict | None], now: float) -> list[Vehicle]` (sync, cache-only)
