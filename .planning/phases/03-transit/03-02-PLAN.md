## Phase Goal

**As** David, **I want to** see how to get moving from where I stand (nearest stops, live departures, approaching vehicles and disruptions), **so that** I am always one step ahead of the next tram, train or bus.

This plan (shared live-push plumbing for slices 2 to 4) makes the runtime and the SSE stream safe for per-client live layers. Today `_poll_loop` publishes `query(0, 0)` as a "global" layer, which would wipe a phone's real departures (RESEARCH Pitfall 1). Exact-location providers now publish a `touch` instead, and the stream turns it into a per-subscriber query behind the same Home gate as `/api/profile`. A rate-guarded `nudge` gives D-23's first-open fetch.

Purpose: departures, vehicles and disruptions can update live without leaking Home or clobbering layers.
Output: runtime.py, assemble.py, stream.py changes plus tests.
</objective>
